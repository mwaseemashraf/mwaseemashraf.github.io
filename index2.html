What?

  • Compute lattice + ionic contributions to the dielectric tensor.
  • Decompose contributions by phonon mode (IR-active modes).
  • Report mode effective charges and per-mode trace contributions.

How?

  • DFPT (IBRION=8, LEPSILON) in VASP.
  • Post-process eigenvectors, masses, and Born charges.
  • Validate convergence vs. ENCUT / k-mesh / LREAL.

Results

  • Per-mode dielectric contributions in a compact table.
  • Identify dominant polar modes and symmetry trends.
  • Reusable pipeline for Zr–Y–O systems and beyond.
Keywords: DFPT, phonons, Born charges, dielectric
Tools: VASP • Python • NumPy
